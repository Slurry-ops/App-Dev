## **About the Movie**
*The Amazing Spider-Man* is a **superhero film** based on the Marvel Comics character **Spider-Man**.  
It was directed by *Marc Webb* and stars **Andrew Garfield** as Peter Parker.

> *"With great power comes great responsibility."*

---

## **Main Characters**
1. **Peter Parker / Spider-Man**  
2. **Gwen Stacy**  
3. **Dr. Curt Connors / The Lizard**  
4. **Aunt May**  

---

## **Why I Like It**
- Stunning visual effects and web-swinging sequences  
- Andrew Garfield’s emotional and relatable portrayal  
- A deeper exploration of Peter’s scientific genius  

---

### **Fun Fact**
Did you know?  
> The film was partly inspired by the *Ultimate Spider-Man* comic series and focused on a more modern take on Peter Parker’s story.
